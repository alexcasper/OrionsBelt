Thermal management is critical for sustained performance on passively cooled devices. When a chip reaches its thermal limit, it reduces clock frequencies to stay within safe operating temperatures. This thermal throttling can cause throughput to drop by thirty percent or more, making it essential to monitor temperatures during benchmarking and to use performance governor for consistent measurements.
